# Apple Music 

This is a walkthrough to downloading your Apple Music listening history. 

## Downloading Data

{{< code-action "Follow the steps below to download your Apple Music listening history." >}} 

> 0. **Go to [Apple’s Data and Privacy page](https://privacy.apple.com/).** 
> 0. **Sign into apple account**
> 0. **Click `Request a copy of your data`**
> 0. **Select `Apple Media Services Information`**
> 0. **Click `Continue`**
> 0. **Click `Complete request`**

Once you get your data in your inbox... (it may take a few days)

> 0. **Unzip `Apple Media Services information` & open the folder**
> 0. **Unzip `Apple_Media_Services` & open the folder**
> 0. **Open `Apple Music Activity`**
> 0. **Your data is in `Apple Music Play Activity.csv`**
